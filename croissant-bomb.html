How it works

  1. A question appears, e.g. "Why did Deng rise to power?" The holder types one piece of evidence and presses Enter.
  2. If it's correct and new, you score points and the croissant passes on. If it's wrong or already said, you're still holding it.
  3. The more precise the evidence, the more it's worth. Names, dates and numbers score best.
  4. If the game doesn't recognise a fair answer, the other player can press Count it to award 1 point.
  5. After each explosion you'll see every piece of evidence nobody said. That's your revision list.
